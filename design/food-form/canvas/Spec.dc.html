Food form — the spec
Issue #88. The contract for #89 (the form) and #93 (serving steps). Every value on this page is read from src/theme/tokens.ts; the preset conversions come from SERVING_PRESETS in src/db/servings.ts and are not repeated in tokens.
1 · The serving picker
Six chips in a fixed 3 × 2 grid. One line of six does not fit a 375 pt phone at a readable size, and a scrolling row would hide Custom… off-screen. Every chip is visible at once.

1 cupSelected
Chip: 44 pt tall, full-width thirds, gap 8 pt. Label type.control, fill foodForm.chipBg, 1 pt foodForm.chipBorder.
Selected: calorie wash foodForm.chipSelectedBg, 1.5 pt foodForm.chipSelectedBorder, bold label type.controlSelected. Shape and weight change as well as colour, so it reads in bad light and for colour-blind users.
Tap: selects at once, fires haptics.servingPicked (selection / EFFECT_TICK). Tapping the selected chip does nothing. No animation on the chip itself.
Default: a new food opens on 100 g. Nutrition labels print per 100 g, so for most packs the serving is already right.
Basis follows the chip: 100 g → weight; 100 ml, 1 cup, 1 tbsp, 1 tsp → volume. The SERVING eyebrow says which (“Weight · grams”), and the nutrition heading flips between “Per 100 g” and “Per 100 ml”. Numbers already typed stay; only their unit changes.
Custom… reads “Custom” once selected. A custom serving belongs to its food and never becomes a chip.
2 · Locked amount vs Custom fields
The same slot under the chips. A preset shows a read-only line; Custom opens three fields in its place, and the form grows downward so the chips never move under your thumb.

Locked, not disabled: padlock glyph, the amount in type.numericLg, and a line that says how to change it. No well, no border, no −/+ — nothing that looks tappable. Tokens foodForm.lockedAmountText, lockedMetaText, lockIcon; row 48 pt.
Custom: Label (text, placeholder “1 scoop”), Measured by (Weight | Volume, the existing segmented.* control), Amount (stepper, tap the value to type — #87). Picking Custom focuses Label, because typing it is the next thing you must do.
Custom starts from the last chip: its basis and amount carry over (1 cup → Custom opens at Volume, 250 ml). Switching is never a reset.
Motion: motion.events.customReveal — 200 ms, height + fade, interruptible. Reduce motion: instant.
Validation: Save with an empty Label outlines it in foodForm.fieldBorderError and prints the reason under it in foodForm.errorText. Never a dialog.
3 · Nutrition and the live preview
kcal and protein only (client ruling). Side-by-side steppers, entered per 100 of the basis, exactly as the pack prints them. The app does the maths for one serving, on the spot.

Steppers: −/+ 44 pt wide, 48 pt tall, gap 10 pt between the pair. The value well is the tap-to-type target (#87); the unit moves up to the label (“Calories kcal”) so the well holds 4 digits. Calories in foodForm.kcalValueText, protein in foodForm.proteinValueText.
Preview: “1 scoop (33 g) = 132 kcal · 25 g protein”, recalculated on every keystroke. 44 pt strip, foodForm.previewBg. When the label already is the amount (100 g), the bracket is dropped: “100 g = 97 kcal · 9 g protein”.
Numbers never truncate; a long label does, with an ellipsis. kcal rounds to a whole number; protein to one decimal under 10 g, whole above. Amounts render through formatGrams / formatMl.
Where it lives: in the sticky footer with Save, so it is always in view — and rides above the keyboard while you type (artboard “Typing”). The footer gains a foodForm.footerDivider hairline once content scrolls under it.

6 · Contract for ui-engineer (#89)
Names the build must use. No colour, size or duration outside these.
Colour: themes[name].color.foodForm.* for fields, eyebrows, chips, the locked read-out, nutrition values, the footer and preview. The Weight | Volume toggle keeps segmented.*; the steppers keep stepper.*; buttons keep button.*. The form no longer borrows searchSheet.*.
Size: size.foodForm.* (chips, fields, locked row, nutrition steppers, preview). Buttons size.button.primaryHit.
Motion / haptics: motion.events.customReveal, haptics.servingPicked.
Steps: interaction.servingSteps[key], keys 100g · 100ml · cup · tbsp · tsp · custom. Recommended to db-engineer: give each SERVING_PRESETS row the same key, so the UI never matches on a display label.
testIDs: ‹id›-serving-‹key› per chip (-serving-cup, -serving-custom), ‹id›-locked-amount, ‹id›-serving-label (Custom only), ‹id›-basis-weight|volume, ‹id›-serving-amount, ‹id›-kcal, ‹id›-protein, ‹id›-preview, ‹id›-save, ‹id›-cancel, ‹id›-error.
Heads-up: test/tap-budget.test.tsx types into -serving-label on a fresh Create form. Under this design that field exists only under Custom, so #89 needs qa-engineer to drop that line (the 100 g default needs no label). The 3 fixed taps do not change.
Save label: in the Create sheet “Save & log ‹serving›” (“Save & log 1 cup”); on /foods/new “Save food”; on /foods/[id] “Save changes” with the history note under it.
