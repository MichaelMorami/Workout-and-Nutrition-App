Arcs — under, on, and over target
Past target a ring must never read as a triumphant full circle, and “over” must survive greyscale. The completed lap drops to arc.kcalOverBase; the overage is a second bright lap with a 3 pt arc.overKnockout cut either side; a 16 pt arc.targetTickIcon crosses the ring at 12 o’clock; and the caption changes from “left” to “over”.
