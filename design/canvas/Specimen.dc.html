Colour & type
Dark is the product; light is the courtesy. Both palettes are generated from the same OKLCH ramp, so every hue keeps its lightness relationship when the theme flips. Every token below is named, has one stated job, and clears 4.5:1 where it carries text.
Dark — primary
Surfaces & lines
bg.canvas #0C0E13 The ground behind every screen. Never pure black — pure black kills the ring glow and bands on OLED.
bg.surface #17191F Cards, the tab bar, the rings panel.
bg.raised #21252B Insets inside a card: stat cells, stepper value, segmented track.
bg.tile #25282F The quick-add tile. One step above the card so it reads as a button, not a row.
bg.press #2C3038 Pressed state for any tile or row. Lightened (dark) / darkened (light) just far enough to read as pressed while every label on top of it still clears 4.5:1.
line.hairline #32353D Every 1 px divider and card border.
line.strong #494D55 Dashed “add” affordances and unselected segment outlines.
Text
text.primary #F5F7FA 17.99:1 on canvas Numbers, food names, screen titles. Everything you actually read.
text.secondary #B4B7BE 9.61:1 on canvas Supporting text you are meant to read, just not first.
text.tertiary #9598A0 5.11:1 on tile Units, timestamps, section eyebrows. The lowest rung — still ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface it lands on.

Type — Archivo, one family, two widths
Archivo carries a variable width axis. Numbers are set in the expanded cut so they read as instrument readouts; words stay at normal width. Two voices, one file, no pairing risk. Tabular figures everywhere — a changing digit must never shift the ones beside it.
1,240 type.displayXlArchivo 33 / 700 / width 118 / −0.022em The number inside each ring, and nothing else.
Today type.displayLgArchivo 25 / 700 / width 116 / −0.018em Screen titles and the headline stat on every chart card.
Bench Press type.titleArchivo 19 / 700 / width 104 Card titles, exercise names, sheet headers.
Chicken Breast type.strongArchivo 16.5 / 650 The quick-add tile name. The one size tuned for arm’s length.
Chicken & Rice type.bodyArchivo 15 / 450 Log rows, settings rows, running text.
80 kg × 8 type.numericArchivo 15 / 700 / width 108 / tabular Every number outside a ring. Tabular so columns never jitter.
Last: 80 kg × 8 · 4 days ago type.labelArchivo 12.5 / 500 Captions, secondary lines, chart footnotes.
Quick add type.microArchivo 10.5 / 650 / +0.14em / caps Section eyebrows and chart labels. Never for anything you must read.
Space
Nine steps, no ad-hoc values. Every gap and every padding on every screen is one of these names.
space.14 pt The tightest gap in the system: a micro label above the number it names.
space.26 pt Inside a control — icon to label, number to unit.
space.38 pt Between a section eyebrow and the thing it heads.
space.410 pt Between quick-add tiles, and between the two chips under them.
space.512 pt The default gap between two cards. If you are unsure, it is this one.
space.616 pt Card padding, and the gutter on Workout, Charts and Settings.
space.720 pt The Today gutter — Today gets more air than any other screen.
space.824 pt Between two settings groups.
space.932 pt Above a screen-level heading in a long scroll.
Radius
Radius encodes size: the bigger the object, the rounder it is. Seven steps, and nothing in between them.
radius.xs8 pt Set-index badges and micro pills inside a row.
radius.sm10 pt A segment inside a segmented track; small utility chips.
radius.md14 pt Steppers, stat cells, portion chips, segmented tracks, primary buttons.
radius.lg18 pt The quick-add tile, the weight and workout chips, settings groups.
radius.xl22 pt Sheets, the rest-timer bar, the account card — the big floating objects.
radius.card24 pt Cards. The largest thing on any screen, so the roundest.
radius.pillfully rounded Anything genuinely pill-shaped: avatars, dots, the search button, Sync now.
